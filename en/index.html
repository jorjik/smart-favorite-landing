What the bot does

A plain "save to favorites" turns into a structured library: author, preview image, summary and category on every note.

🔗

Links, with author and image

Threads, X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, articles. The bot pulls the title, the author's handle and a preview - even from JS-rendered, login-walled pages.

🗂

Categories take care of themselves

AI files notes under your own category list and learns from manual moves. Move one note to Work - the next ones go there too.

🔎

Search by a phrase

"where is that post about token optimization" - and the note is there. It searches the text, the summary and the OCR'd text from photos.

🎬

Video: download or summarize

For a YouTube or TikTok link the bot offers two buttons: download the clip or write a summary. Long videos are saved with a link and a cover.

🎙

Voice notes and photos with text

Voice notes are transcribed into notes. For a photo the bot asks: extract the text or save it as is.

⏰

Reminders and referrals

The Remind button on a card sends you the note back in Telegram when it's due. Invite a friend: they save 3 notes - you get 21 days of Pro.
