X (Twitter)

How to export X bookmarks and likes and move them to Telegram

X packs all bookmarks and likes into one data archive. The files bookmark.js and like.js contain every post ID; upload the ZIP or the files themselves and each post becomes a note.

~10 minutes plus archive wait · free · no OAuth, just a file

  1. Request your X archive

    Open Settings and privacy → Your account → Download an archive of your data. Confirm your password and wait for the email.

  2. Download and unzip the archive

    The email usually arrives within 24 hours. Unzip it and open the data folder.

  3. Find bookmark.js and like.js

    data/bookmark.js holds bookmarks, data/like.js holds likes. You can upload the ZIP or just these files.

  4. Upload to the mini app

    Open Smart Favorite → Import, choose X and upload the ZIP or the JS files. The parser strips the window.YTD... wrapper.

  5. Review the batch

    Bookmarks become x.com/i/status/<id> links; likes also get the post text as a title. Remove the noise and move the keepers to a rubric.

What gets imported

  • Bookmarked posts, as links to the original posts.
  • Liked posts with their text as the note title when the archive has it.
  • Deleted or private posts that the archive still mentions.
  • Direct messages, followers and media files.

Common questions

How long does the X archive take?

Usually under 24 hours. X emails a download link when the archive is ready.

Why are some likes missing?

Deleted posts, private accounts and duplicates are skipped. The result screen shows the exact counts.

Do I need to unzip the archive?

No. Upload the ZIP as is, or unzip it and upload bookmark.js and like.js separately.

Move your X bookmarks and likes in a couple of minutes

The file is not stored on disk, duplicates are skipped, and the whole import can be undone.
