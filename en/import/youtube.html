How to export YouTube Watch later and liked videos and move them to Telegram

Google Takeout can give you Watch later, Liked videos and your own playlists as CSV files. Upload them to the mini app and every video becomes a note with its title.

~10 minutes plus Takeout wait · free · no OAuth, just a file

  1. Open Google Takeout

    Go to myaccount.google.com → Data & privacy → Download your data.

  2. Select only YouTube playlists

    Deselect everything, then choose YouTube and YouTube Music → playlists. Pick the CSV format if Google asks.

  3. Create the export and download it

    Takeout emails a link when the archive is ready. Download and unzip it.

  4. Find the playlist CSV

    Inside Takeout/YouTube and YouTube Music/playlists/ look for Watch later.csv and Liked videos.csv.

  5. Upload to the mini app

    Open Smart Favorite → Import, choose YouTube and upload the CSV or the whole ZIP. The Video ID column becomes a watch link and the title becomes the note title.

What gets imported

  • Watch later, Liked videos and your own playlists, as video links.
  • Video titles from the CSV.
  • Watch history and search history.
  • Private or deleted videos that no longer open.

Common questions

Which Takeout format should I choose?

CSV is the easiest: each row has a Video ID and a title. JSON also works with the universal file parser.

Why are some videos missing?

Private, deleted and region-blocked videos are skipped, and duplicates are counted once.

Can I import one playlist only?

Yes. Upload just that playlist CSV, or import everything and use the review screen to remove the rest in bulk.

Move your YouTube playlists in a couple of minutes

The file is not stored on disk, duplicates are skipped, and the whole import can be undone.
