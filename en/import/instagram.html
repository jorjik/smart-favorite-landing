How to export saved posts from Instagram and move them to Telegram

Instagram gives you every saved post and reel in one ZIP. Inside is JSON with links; upload the archive to the mini app and each link becomes a note.

~10 minutes · free · no OAuth, just a file

  1. Open Accounts Center

    In the Instagram app: profile → menu ☰ → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions. The path is the same on phone and desktop.

  2. Create an export

    Tap Export your information (older versions say Download your information) → Create export. Pick the Instagram account if you have several.

  3. Select only Saved

    Clear every other checkbox and tick Saved. Choose All time and the JSON format. The archive stays small and arrives faster.

  4. Wait for the email and download the ZIP

    Instagram emails a link to the address on the account: usually minutes to a couple of days. Download the archive; do not unzip it.

  5. Upload the ZIP to the mini app

    Open Smart Favorite → Import, choose Instagram and upload the whole ZIP. your_instagram_activity/saved/saved_posts.json is found automatically.

What gets imported

  • Posts, reels and videos from Saved, as links to the original posts.
  • Captions when the export contains them, as the note text.
  • Deleted posts and service records: their links do not open and are skipped.
  • Stories and direct messages — they are not part of Saved.

Common questions

Do I need to unzip the archive?

No. Upload the ZIP as is: the parser finds saved_posts.json inside your_instagram_activity/saved/.

Why are there fewer notes than saved posts?

Some records are deleted posts, ads or duplicates inside the file. They are skipped, and the result screen shows how many were added, already existed or were dropped.

The file is large — is that normal?

A healthy Saved archive is a few megabytes. If the ZIP is over 25 MB, the export probably included other sections: create a new one with only Saved selected.

Move your saved posts in a couple of minutes

The file is not stored on disk, duplicates are skipped, and the whole import can be undone.
